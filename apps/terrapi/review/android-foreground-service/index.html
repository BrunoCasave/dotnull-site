Terrapi: user-initiated app restrictions

Google Play review demonstration of FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE. Recorded on a Pixel 6a with Android 16, Terrapi 1.0.0 (20), on 20 October 2026. No sign-in is needed to watch.

Open or download the original recording (MP4, approximately 53 MB)

What the recording demonstrates

Approximate timeAction and result
00:10–00:13The user selects a 15-minute focus session, enables app restrictions and starts the session.
00:15–00:18The user opens Calculator, an app they previously selected. Terrapi displays its focus restriction overlay.
00:19–00:22The user returns to Terrapi and explicitly ends the focus session.
00:23–00:29The user reopens Calculator without changing the selected-app settings. The restriction overlay is gone.

The foreground service checks the foreground app during a user-started restriction session and shows an overlay only for selected apps. The recording demonstrates initiation, restriction and release; it does not show the Android notification panel. The recording has no audio.
