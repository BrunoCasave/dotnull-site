Privacy policy

About this policy

Next Time Alarm is an app from dotnull.co.jp. This policy describes the current development build. Contact tomorrow-alarm@dotnull.co.jp.

Information on your device

Group and alarm names, weekdays, times, enabled settings, date overrides, reservation identifiers and recovery information stay on your device. We do not read system Clock alarms, device location, contacts or health data. There are no accounts, proprietary servers, cloud sync or analytics SDKs.

OS scheduling and backup

Names and scheduled times are passed to the OS alarm service to deliver your alarms. Android keeps minimal alarm data available before the first unlock after reboot and excludes app data from OS backup. The iOS templates, overrides and recovery receipts are excluded from backup. On iOS, the app checks reservations returned by the OS. On Android, it checks registration results and local records. Records alone do not prove an OS alarm remains scheduled. The new method uses no countdown card before ringing. It books the original time once, 7 days after the changed date. Without restoration by a stop, that weekday has no alarms from day 14 onward. Other weekdays continue. Check schedules after travel or clock changes; a time zone change blocks restoration.

Advertising and network use

Ad requests and consent forms are disabled in the current build. Google Mobile Ads and UMP are included and used only on Home when enabled by a development flag. When enabled, Google may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics. Alarm names, weekdays and routine times are not sent to the ad SDK. When ads are enabled, Google may infer an approximate location from the IP address. If live ads are enabled, providing a birth year is optional and the entered year is not saved. Live ads are not shown to minors or people whose age is unknown.

Choices and permissions

When ads are enabled, required consent is checked and a privacy choices entry appears where required. Refusing consent never restricts alarms. This build does not request tracking authorization and requests non-personalized ads when ads are enabled. OS settings control alarm, notification and full-screen permissions.

Deletion and support

Use More to delete all data. OS cancellations are confirmed before deleting local settings. Email addresses and messages sent to support are used to reply and investigate issues, and kept only as needed. Do not send secrets or unnecessary personal alarm labels.

Website and updates

This static site contains no ads, analytics scripts, contact form or custom cookies. The hosting and email providers may process operational access information. We review this policy and its date when features or SDKs change.
