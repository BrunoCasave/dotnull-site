Support

Before you start

Only alarms created in this app are supported. It cannot read or edit system Clock alarms. Keep your usual alarm until reliability testing is complete.

Change next time only

Change only the group’s next scheduled day. Today is eligible before its first alarm; otherwise the next regular day is selected. Set earlier alarms before their new time.

Create a group

Save a group name, repeat weekdays, labels and times. The first version supports 24 groups and 24 alarms total. Turning a group off differs from taking the next occurrence off. The device may allow fewer alarms depending on available system capacity.

Change the next occurrence

Change only the group’s next scheduled day. Today is eligible before its first alarm; otherwise the next regular day is selected. Set earlier alarms before their new time. iPhone: stop each delayed alarm on the OS alarm screen to restore its regular schedule. A later regular alarm stop can also restore items after their changed time. iPhone: after an earlier alarm, stop a later regular alarm from this app on the OS alarm screen, after the original time has passed. This restores weekly schedules for eligible items. iPhone: the skipped occurrence does not ring. Stop a later regular alarm from this app on the OS alarm screen to restore weekly schedules for items whose original time has passed. The new method uses no countdown card before ringing. It books the original time once, 7 days after the changed date. Without restoration by a stop, that weekday has no alarms from day 14 onward. Other weekdays continue. Check schedules after travel or clock changes; a time zone change blocks restoration.

Cancel or stop remaining alarms

Editing closes at the earliest original or changed time. Later changes cannot restore past alarms. Android can stop only the rest of today. On iPhone, turning a group OFF cancels its future alarms too.

If Android does not ring

Targets Android8+. Check exact-alarm, notification and full-screen permissions separately. Power off, Settings force-stop, revoked permissions and battery restrictions can prevent delivery. Removing the app from recents is different from force-stop. Check the OS alarm volume. Unanswered ringing ends after 10 minutes.

Time changes

Normal schedules use device local time. On Android, missing DST times move to the next valid time; repeated times use the earlier offset once. Review schedules after travel or clock changes. The Changed iOS alarms use fixed absolute dates. Check schedules after travel or clock changes. Restoration is blocked after a time zone change.

Report a problem

Email your OS, device model, app version, expected date and time, actual result, lock state and permission status. Do not include keys, passwords or personal alarm labels. After changing time zones, turn the group OFF to clear its override, check local times, then turn it ON. This also cancels future alarms temporarily.
