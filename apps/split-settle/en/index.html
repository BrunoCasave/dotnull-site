How to use

Split Settle

01

Create a settlement

Create a group for your trip or meal. Add names and choose one currency.

02

Add payment

Enter the amount, payer and people sharing each expense. See the remaining balance immediately.

03

Share result

Share text or an image. Record actual repayments after handing over the money.

Details

Use one of eight currencies per group, divide each payment equally among selected people, and record money actually handed over. Works offline for records and calculations. Free with a home-screen banner ad. No currency conversion or money transfers.

Privacy policy

Records are stored in the local database. Your sharing actions and OS backups can send copies outside this device. Restoration is not guaranteed. The advertising SDK may communicate device, network and ad interaction data. Names, amounts, notes and settlement history are not supplied to it. No account or custom analytics is used.
