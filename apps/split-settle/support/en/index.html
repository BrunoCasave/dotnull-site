How to use

Create a group with two or more distinct names. Add each payment and select who shares it. Share the result, then record money only after actually handing it over. Correct an expense if the original payer receives a store refund. Other refund flows are not supported.

Record money handed over

Save only after actually handing over money. This app does not send or verify money.

Delete

Data removal: delete individual payment and transfer records from History. Uninstalling removes local app data; copies in OS backups or recipients’ devices may remain.
