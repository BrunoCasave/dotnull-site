Privacy policy

Records are stored in the local database. Your sharing actions and OS backups can send copies outside this device. Restoration is not guaranteed. The advertising SDK may communicate device, network and ad interaction data. Names, amounts, notes and settlement history are not supplied to it. No account or custom analytics is used.

Advertisement

Advertising services may collect IP address (approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ads, fraud prevention and service operation. We disable first-party advertising IDs on iOS and remove Android AD_ID permission. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. Use Ad privacy choices when available.

Delete

Data removal: delete individual payment and transfer records from History. Uninstalling removes local app data; copies in OS backups or recipients’ devices may remain.
