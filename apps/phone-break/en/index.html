Choose your time

Set the first reminder for 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes and grant the required permissions.

03

Across your apps. One reminder to pause.

A reminder, without a lockout
Get a prompt to put your phone down without forcibly blocking your apps.
A fresh start after a break
After the first reminder, receive another every 10 minutes of use. A break of at least two minutes starts a new session. On iPhone, this is estimated.
No account needed
Monitoring settings and usage state stay on your device. Development builds include test ads.

How time is measured

iPhone

On iPhone, continuous use is estimated from apps and websites recorded by Screen Time. It is not an exact screen-on timer; records and notifications can be delayed.

Android

On Android, time is counted while the screen is on and unlocked. Battery settings or force-stopping the app can interrupt monitoring.
