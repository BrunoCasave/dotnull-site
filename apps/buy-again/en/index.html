Did you like it last time?
Buy Again? saves product photos, personal ratings and purchase notes so your last experience can help with your next shopping trip.

How it works

Photos help with the typing.

01

Take a photo

Photograph a label or package, choose an existing image, or enter text manually.

02

Check the suggestions

Compare suggested fields with the original photo and correct what is needed.

03

Save and revisit

Keep photos and details on your device. Search for them before your next purchase.

Features

Let your last purchase guide the next one.

A saved product with the same barcode can show its previous rating. New product suggestions come from package text, not an online product database.

  • Read package text to suggest the product name, brand and size
  • Rate products as Buy again, Maybe or Not again
  • Keep a name, size, barcode, notes and multiple photos
  • Search product names or notes and filter by rating
  • Find a saved product’s previous rating with its barcode
  • A Google AdMob banner appears at the bottom. The ad SDK sends device information and other data to Google for ads. Photos, model numbers, product names, ratings and notes are never added to ad requests.

Your notes stay on this device.

No account is needed. Photos and notes are stored in the app and are not uploaded by the app. Deleting the app also deletes your notes. There is no cloud sync or data transfer feature.

Google ML Kit may also send usage, performance and diagnostic data to Google. See the privacy policy for details. Notes work even when ad consent or network access is unavailable.
