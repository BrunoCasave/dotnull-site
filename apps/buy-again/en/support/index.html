Support

How do I add a record?

Choose Add a photo, take or select a photo, review and correct the suggestions, and save. You can also enter text manually.

The text was not read correctly

Use good light, enlarge the text in the frame and reduce reflections and tilt. You can edit suggestions or save just the photo.

How do I edit or delete?

Open a record from the list and use Edit to change its fields and photos. Deleted records cannot be restored.

What if I change devices or uninstall?

There is no app-provided sync, backup or migration. Keep important information separately. OS or device transfer is not guaranteed.

Where are ad settings?

Consent screens appear where required. Ad privacy options appear in About this app when a choice-change entry is needed. Records work without ads.

Photos help with the typing.

A saved product with the same barcode can show its previous rating. New product suggestions come from package text, not an online product database.

Contact us

Please include the app name, app and OS versions, and the steps that caused the problem. You do not need to attach photos or private records.
