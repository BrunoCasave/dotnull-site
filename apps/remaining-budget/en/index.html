Remaining
The remaining amount is based only on entries you record. It is not your bank balance or a guarantee that you can spend it. Refunds restore your budget; they are not income tracking. Periods do not renew or carry over automatically.

Advertising and privacy choices
The app is free and uses one small home-screen banner. Advertising is hidden while typing or editing. Privacy choices, when required, are available in Settings. Consent or network failures do not prevent recording expenses.

Records on your device

No account needed.

Budget amounts, currencies, dates, expenses, refunds, notes and settings are stored on your device to provide the app’s features. No account or custom cloud sync is used. We do not send these records to our servers or to the advertising SDK. No analytics SDK is added.

Data is saved on your device. OS backups and device transfer may include it, but restoration is not guaranteed. Deleting the app may delete your data. There is no account or cloud restore service.
