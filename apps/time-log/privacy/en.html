Privacy policy

Records on your device

Activity names, session timestamps and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, proprietary server, sync or analytics. OS backups and device transfers may include these records; restoration is not guaranteed.

Ads and consent

Google AdMob and UMP provide a small banner. The SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inference), device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not send activity names, session timestamps or history to the ad SDK. Consent information is refreshed at launch, with a form when required. Change available choices in Settings under Ad privacy choices. We do not request tracking permission. Recording remains available when ads or consent requests fail.

Contact and deletion

Operator: DOTNULL Co.,Ltd. Your email address and inquiry are used to respond and provide support. You do not need to send personal activity history. Delete records in History. Permanently deleting an activity also deletes its history; archiving preserves it. Manage OS backup copies through your OS. See Google’s privacy policy for Google’s data processing.
