How it works

Record personal activities with Start and Stop. Time includes app closure, screen lock and device restarts until you stop. Today and Monday-based weeks use your current device time zone. Changing zones can redistribute past daily totals without changing stored UTC instants or total duration. Device clock changes cannot always be detected. Errors from undetectable offline clock changes are not guaranteed against. Review and correct long sessions in history.

Correcting records

In History, add, edit or delete past sessions and change their activity. Future times, ends before or equal to starts, and overlapping sessions are rejected. Adjacent sessions are allowed. Stop the active session before archiving or deleting its activity.

Records on your device

Records stay on your device. No account, proprietary server or sync. OS backup and migration may include records, but restoration is not guaranteed.
