Features

Record time spent creating, studying or reading. See today’s and this week’s totals, starting Monday, on the home screen. Switching activities ends one session and starts the next at the same instant. Add, correct or delete past records in history. Records stay on your device. No account needed. Free, with a small banner ad.

No account. Records on your device.

Activity names, session timestamps and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, proprietary server, sync or analytics. OS backups and device transfers may include these records; restoration is not guaranteed.
