Backups and changing devices

On your old device, choose Save backup in Settings and save the file somewhere safe outside the device. On the new device, choose Import backup in Settings and select that file.

Import keeps existing items and avoids duplicate copies of matching items. There is no automatic sync. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them securely. Keep the old app until you have checked your items on the new device.
